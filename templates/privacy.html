Privacy Policy

Effective: June 1, 2026

Cloud Orbit ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

Learning Progress Data: We store your lesson progress, XP, energy shields, badges, achievements, and quiz results locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to sync it via a cloud service in the future.

No Personal Information: Cloud Orbit does not require account creation. We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any other personally identifiable information.

No Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your learning progress data is used exclusively to provide the core game experience: tracking completed lessons, managing energy shields, awarding XP and badges, and enabling streak tracking. All computation happens on-device.

3. Data Storage & Security

All progress data is stored in a local JSON file (progress.json) on your device. For the web version, this is stored in your browser's local storage. No data is transmitted to external servers. We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your local data.

4. Children's Privacy

Cloud Orbit is designed for learners of all ages. Since we do not collect personal information, the app is compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR-K (General Data Protection Regulation for Children).

5. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control. You can delete your progress data at any time by clearing the app's data or removing progress.json. No data retention or deletion requests are necessary because we don't hold your data.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be reflected in the app and on our website. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at github.com/Cybranfox/CloudOpsTestApp.
