## Full Description
Cloud Orbit turns cloud and DevOps certification prep into an epic RPG adventure. Battle through 78 scenario-based challenges across 5 platforms — AWS, Kubernetes, Docker, Ansible, and Terraform — guided by Zap, your animated AI mascot.

**What makes Cloud Orbit different:**

- **RPG Battle System** — Answer correctly to attack, earn XP, and collect relics. Get it wrong and lose energy shields. Just like Slay the Spire meets Duolingo.
- **5 Live Platforms** — AWS (24 lessons), Kubernetes (20), Docker (10), Ansible (12), Terraform (12). New platforms added regularly.
- **Roguelite Depth** — Earn technique cards between battles, activate relics that change gameplay, climb ascension levels for harder challenges.
- **Gem Economy** — Earn gems from streaks and victories. Spend them on hints and shield refills.
- **Daily & Weekly Challenges** — New seeded challenges every day and week. Same questions for everyone — compete with friends.
- **Works Offline** — Full PWA support. No account needed. All progress stored locally. Learn anywhere, anytime.
- **Zero Data Collection** — No analytics, no tracking, no account required. Your progress stays on your device.

Built with Python, Flask, and Capacitor. Open source under MIT license.
